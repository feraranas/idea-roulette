  - heading "Subcontractor Invoice Reconciliation Tool" [level=1]
  - paragraph: Upload subcontractor invoices and original contracts to automatically flag discrepancies in rates, quantities, and scope changes. Generates summary reports showing approved vs. billed amounts, helping general contractors catch billing errors that typically cost 3-8% of project value when missed.
  - button "Hide one pager" [expanded]
  - paragraph: The Subcontractor Invoice Reconciliation Tool serves project managers and accounts payable teams at general contractors managing $10-100 million in annual subcontractor spend. These professionals, typically managing 5-20 active projects with 50-200 subcontractors, lose 3-8% of project margin to billing errors, unauthorized change orders, and rate discrepancies that slip through manual review processes. The pressure to process invoices quickly for cash flow management conflicts with the need for detailed verification, creating an environment where expensive errors are inevitable.
  - paragraph: The core value proposition is recovering lost margin by catching billing errors before payment, with most customers seeing ROI within the first month of use. By automatically comparing every line item on subcontractor invoices against original contract terms, the tool identifies discrepancies that human reviewers miss in the rush to process hundreds of invoices monthly. The system transforms invoice review from a rushed, error-prone task into a systematic verification process that takes minutes instead of hours while catching 10x more discrepancies.
  - paragraph: The comprehensive feature set starts with intelligent document parsing that extracts data from PDF invoices and contracts using advanced OCR with construction-specific training. The contract database maintains current rates, terms, and approved change orders for every subcontractor, with automatic version control and audit trails. The reconciliation engine performs line-by-line matching of invoice items against contract terms, flagging rate variances, quantity overages, and unauthorized work. The discrepancy dashboard prioritizes issues by dollar value and provides one-click drill-down to source documents.
  - paragraph: The approval workflow routes flagged invoices to appropriate reviewers with annotated discrepancies and suggested resolutions. The dispute documentation generator creates professional variance reports for subcontractor discussions, complete with contract references and calculation details. The analytics module tracks error patterns by subcontractor, project, and category, identifying problematic vendors and common billing issues. The integration layer exports approved amounts to accounting systems and maintains synchronization with project management platforms.
  - paragraph: For controllers watching margins erode and project managers drowning in paperwork, this tool transforms invoice reconciliation from a necessary evil into a profit center that pays for itself with the first caught error.

  - heading "Prototype overview" [level=1]
  - paragraph: This single-page prototype demonstrates how a general contractor would reconcile a subcontractor invoice against its original contract to catch billing errors before payment. The user lands on the default home page and can either drag-and-drop (mock) an invoice and a contract or click “Use sample data” to prefill the experience. With one click on “Run Reconciliation,” the page generates a summary of billed vs. approved amounts, highlights variance totals, and shows a detailed line-item table that flags discrepancies in rate, quantity, and scope. Filters allow users to quickly view only problematic items (e.g., rate or quantity issues). A side-panel “Discrepancy Report” provides a concise, shareable summary for dispute documentation. The entire experience is responsive, keyboard-accessible, and uses the existing design system with Tailwind styling, avoiding gradients and purple. All data is local and mocked; no uploads, persistence, or backend services are involved.
